# Worked example: used furniture

**Fictional walkthrough; prices are illustrative.**

Request: “Find a solid-wood desk under USD 200 in Austin, no wider than 48 inches, pickup this weekend.”

1. Confirm depth, whether stairs/transport are manageable, acceptable wear, and search radius. A weekend pickup preference must be checked with the seller by the user; it is not a URL filter.
2. In the signed-in browser, confirm `austin`, then run two searches with a pause of at least 2 seconds:

```text
https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/austin/search/?query=solid%20wood%20desk&maxPrice=200&category=home&deliveryMethod=local_pick_up&radius=20
https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/austin/search/?query=writing%20desk&maxPrice=200&category=home&deliveryMethod=local_pick_up&radius=20&sortBy=creation_time_descend
```

3. Check accepted filters/location, extract the first pages, and deduplicate. Inspect up to three item pages for dimensions, material, joint condition, photos, and pickup access. Exclude a 60-inch desk even if cheap; do not assume “wood” means solid wood.
4. Fictional ranking: “A 46-inch desk at USD 140 is the best candidate if the underside photos confirm solid wood; cosmetic scratches appear manageable. A USD 75 desk has missing dimensions and visible swelling, so its lower price does not establish value.” Compare equivalent local asking prices and disclose uncertainty.
5. Draft questions: “What are the exact width/depth and construction material?” “Any odors, pests, loose joints, or stairs at pickup?”

Include durable item links in a real report; these fictional candidates have none. If a wall appears, stop and report partial results. See [deal checklist](../docs/deal-checklist.md).
