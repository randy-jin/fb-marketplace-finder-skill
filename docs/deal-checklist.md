# Deal checklist

## Is it good value?

- Confirm the exact model, dimensions, included parts, condition, and compatibility. Missing evidence stays unknown.
- Compare a few equivalent local used listings; asking prices are not proof of completed-sale value. State currency and comparison date/source. Include repairs, missing accessories, transport, and inspection costs.
- Check freshness and sold/pending status. A price cut alone does not prove a bargain. Do not calculate precise travel distance from a city label.
- Prefer a slightly higher complete, inspectable item over an apparently cheap item with expensive unknowns. Give a short reason for each ranking and explain what would change it.

## Scam and quality red flags

- Implausibly low price, reused/stock photos, vague or contradictory details, duplicate listings in distant cities, pressure to act immediately.
- New or empty seller profile combined with other warning signs; profile age alone does not establish trust or fraud.
- Deposits before inspection, gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfers, payment links, requests for verification codes, or pressure to leave the platform.
- Refusal to provide original photos, demonstrate the item, allow inspection, or explain ownership. Shipping claims unsupported by the item page.
- Tires/wheels: verify tire size, wheel dimensions, bolt pattern, offset, hub bore, brake clearance, TPMS, tread depth, date codes, cracks, repairs, and uneven wear. Confirm against the specific car's requirements; appearance is insufficient.
- Furniture: check dimensions, material, joints, odors, pests, stairs/access, and pickup costs.
- Cars: check VIN consistency, title/lien status, service history, mileage units, accident history, and an independent inspection. Seller claims and listing filters do not verify these facts.

A flag is a reason to investigate or reject, not proof of a scam. Suggest questions for the user to ask; the agent must never contact sellers or send money. Recommend inspection before payment and a suitable public pickup location when practical.
